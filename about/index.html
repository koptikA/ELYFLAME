Rhythmic gymnastics

Five apparatus.
A world of movement.

Rhythmic gymnastics brings together movement, music and apparatus. An Olympic sport since 1984, it makes room for both athletic skill and artistic expression.

  • Rope

    Jump. Find a rhythm.

  • Hoop

    Roll. Turn. Explore space.

  • Ball

    Balance. Throw. Catch.

  • Clubs

    Two hands. One movement.

  • Ribbon

    Draw a shape in the air.
