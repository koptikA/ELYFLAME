04 / A confident first step

Little questions.
Big beginnings.

A new activity comes with questions.
Here’s a little clarity before you visit.

Ask us anything ↗
Does my child need experience?

No experience is needed to explore the recreational program. At the trial, the coach assesses age, skills, abilities, and strength to recommend a starting point.

How much is a trial lesson?

The trial lesson is $10. Contact the academy to confirm availability and payment arrangements before your visit.

What if we already have gymnastics experience?

Tell us about your child’s previous rhythmic gymnastics training when you get in touch. The trial helps the coach assess the right program and level.

Can teens and adults join?

Stretching & Flexibility is a separate program open to teens, adults, and athletes from other sports. Ask the academy about the current schedule.

How do we register after the trial?

After the assessment, contact the academy for the recommended class, schedule, and enrollment instructions.
